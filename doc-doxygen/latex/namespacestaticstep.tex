Subroutine to propagate one gradient step. 

First opens protocoll files and sets several variables. The main step is performed for blocks of same quantum number m separately. The Hamiltonian is applied to the wave function and the Hamiltonian matrix is constructed. All variances are accumulated. After damping the iteration step is performed. One extra subiteration can be performed. Then the wave functions are diagonalized and orthonormalized. The new densities are constructed (only {\ttfamily rho} for constraint iterations). In the next optional step, the constraints are applied and all densities are accumulated. Orthonormalization is performed again. Now the pairing weights and occupations are updated, optionally fields are printed and finally the new mean fields are evaluated.

Calculation of Q-\/corrective step and update of Lagrange parameter {\ttfamily qlambda}. 

Various strategies to deduce \textquotesingle{}qcorr\textquotesingle{} and \textquotesingle{}qlambda\textquotesingle{} can be chosen\+:
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item ifqq = 0\+: difference of damped Q, local estimate of Delta Q
\item ifqq = 1\+: difference of damped Q, exact variances of damped Q
\item ifqq = 2\+: from raw Q and local estimate of raw Delta Q
\item ifqq = -\/1\+: augmented Lagrangian, requires proper setting of \textquotesingle{}caugmlagr\textquotesingle{}, still in testing stage. The routine updates the corrective array \char`\"{}wrk4\char`\"{}. Note\+: option \textquotesingle{}ifqq=1\textquotesingle{} requires smaller \textquotesingle{}c0\textquotesingle{}, typically around 0.\+2-\/0.\+3.
