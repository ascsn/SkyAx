Computes the pair density, potential, energy and gaps. 

The pairing density is given by \[ \xi_q(\vec r) = \sum_{\alpha\in q} \sum_{s} u_{\alpha} v_{\alpha} \psi_{\overline{\alpha}}(\vec{r},s)\psi_{\alpha}(\vec{r},s) \] the pairing potential by \[ U_{\rm pair}(\vec r)=-V_\mathrm{pair,q} \xi_q(\vec r) \left[1 -\frac{\rho}{\rho_{0,\mathrm{pair}}}\right] \] the pairing energy by \[ E_{\rm pair}=\frac{1}{4} \sum_{q\in\{p,n\}}V_\mathrm{pair,q} \int \D^3r |\xi_q(\vec r)|^2 \left[1 -\frac{\rho(\vec r)}{\rho_{0,\mathrm{pair}}}\right]\] and the pairing gap by \[ \Delta_k=\int\D^3r V_P(\vec r)\left|\phi_k(\vec r)\right|^2\] {\ttfamily ipair} is used here to indicate the switch to surface pairing\+: 5,6\+: volume pairing; 7,8\+: surface pairing. \index{pairin@{pairin}!lm2def@{lm2def}}

Calculates the contribution from the Skyrme residual interaction to {\ttfamily lambda\+\_\+2} in Lipkin-\/\+Nogami Pairing. 

Computes the response-\/densities to the particle number operator using pairing weights from {\ttfamily weight}. The response Hamiltonian is constructed from the Skyrme-\/\+Energy-\/functional, for the details see \cite{Reinhard1992} \cite{Reinhard1992a}. The Lipkin-\/\+Nogami does only proton-\/proton pairing and neutron-\/neutron pairing. Thus only the Z$^\wedge$2 and N$^\wedge$2 response is required. The procedure is extensively commented for the first term {\ttfamily xpotp} and {\ttfamily xpotn}. The other terms run analogously. Results are returned via
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item {\ttfamily rspln} = $ u^2v^2 $ response energy to be used in Lipk.\+Nogami.
\item {\ttfamily suv2r} = $ \sum u^2v^2 $, to be used in Lipkin-\/\+Nogami.
\end{DoxyItemize}
