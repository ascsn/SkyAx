\subsubsection{Detailed Description}
This module contains the routines to compute the mean-\/field Hamiltonian, applying it to a wave function, the Gram-\/\+Schmidt orthonormalization and other helper functions for the damped gradient step.

Computes stabilizing potential for positive-\/energy states. The resulting potential is returned on array \textquotesingle{}potadd\textquotesingle{} via module \textquotesingle{}fields\textquotesingle{}. 

A deformation dependent \char`\"{}coordinate\char`\"{} is deduced from the density of surface states, accumulated on {\ttfamily potadd} as \[ \texttt{effrad} = \max(0,-\log(\texttt{potadd}/\texttt{rhocri})) \] \[ \texttt{rhocri} = \texttt{rho0}\cdot\texttt{rhocut} \] Where {\ttfamily rhocri} (recommended 0.\+08 or 0.\+07) sets the fractional density at the outer surface. The slope of \textquotesingle{}effrad\textquotesingle{} is then scaled to 1 and the desired slope {\ttfamily stslop} of the additional potential is reconstructed.

The additional potential is switched with the particle energy as \[\theta_w=\begin{array}{lr} 0 & \mbox{for }\epsilon < -\texttt{wext}\\ (1-|\epsilon|)/\texttt{wext} & \mbox{for }-\texttt{wext} < \epsilon < 0\\ 1 & \mbox{for }0 < \epsilon \end{array} \] The energy-\/width {\ttfamily wext} is choosen as $ \texttt{wext} = \epsilon_F/2 $
