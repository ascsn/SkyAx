Initializes the constraint with Wood-\/\+Saxon damping at the outer surface. 

The damping factor as such is stored in {\ttfamily fda}.
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item Option {\ttfamily iflin=0}\+: quadrupole as such is damped
\item Option {\ttfamily iflin=1}\+: linear asymptotic and softened damping.
\end{DoxyItemize}

The linear asymtotic constraint is\+: \[ Q=c\cdot \sqrt{1+\frac{4z^2}{c}}-c-\frac{r^2}{\sqrt{1+4z^2/c}} \] where $ c=16\cdot z_{\rm switch}^2 $ and {\ttfamily z\+\_\+switch} indicates the z-\/value where the quadratic behaviour switches to linear behaviour. {\ttfamily z\+\_\+switch} is determined as $ \texttt{swifac} \cdot 1.2 \cdot A^{1/3}$ and {\ttfamily swifac} comes from input. The {\ttfamily z} is taken relative to the center-\/of-\/mass {\ttfamily zcm}. Finally, the Q is damped to limit negative values from below which is done later on by \[ \lambda \cdot Q \rightarrow \frac{\lambda \cdot Q}{\texttt{max}(\lambda \cdot Q,\texttt{VQlim})} \] \index{constr@{constr}!prep\+\_\+qsoll@{prep\+\_\+qsoll}}

Prepares pool of wanted deformations by translating dimensionless deformation to multipole moments. Determines also values for {\ttfamily nz} and {\ttfamily nr}. 

Convert deformation, using the relations\+:
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item $ Q_{\rm cartesian} = 1.09 \cdot A^{5/3} \cdot \beta_2 $
\item $ Q_{40} = 0.495 \cdot A^{7/3} \cdot \beta_4 $
\item $ Q_{30} = 0.41 \cdot A^{2} \cdot \beta_3 $
\item $ Q_{20} = 0.34 \cdot A^{5/3} \cdot \beta_2 $
