Detailed Description

This module contains routines and variables to solve the Coulomb problem.

The Coulomb field is determined from the charge density by the Poisson equation

Although the given density is well localized, the resulting Coulomb field is long ranged. The behavior at large distance is determined by the multipole moments

We deal with axially symmetric systems which have only non-zero moments for and ignore in the following.

To cope with the long range behavior, we separate the problem into a longe-range part and a short-range part.

where the long-range reference densities are defined as

The choice of the width parameter is The reference radii are the smaller of the extensions in and direction and the larger one.

The Coulomb problem for the reference densities becomes a simple radial Poisson equation which can be solved in inexpensive manner by a couple of radial integrals. The solution for the long-range part is thus composed as

There remains the Poisson equation for the short-range part which is simplified by the boundary conditions , i.e. that the field vanishes everywhere at the bounds of the box. We solve it by damped gradient iteration similarly as for the s.p. wavefunctions.

Solves the Poisson equation for charged particles.

Uses the FALR technique, see [1] . An exactly solvable long-range part of the Coulomb problem is separated and the remaining short-range part, which is then free of singularities in k-space, is solved by FFT.
